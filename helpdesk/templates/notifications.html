How to set it up

Settings are environment variables on the server (on Render: the service's Environment tab). Nothing secret is kept in the database or shown here.

Chat: in the Google Chat space choose Apps & integrations, Webhooks, copy the address into HD_CHAT_WEBHOOK. Email: HD_SMTP_HOST, HD_SMTP_PORT (587 or 465), HD_SMTP_USER, HD_SMTP_PASS, HD_SMTP_FROM. Optional HD_BASE_URL for the links in messages.
